# Embedded difficulties → spec sentences → hidden tests (sealed during the experiment)

Every row: the requirement as planted in SPEC.md (quoted, with section), why teams usually get it wrong, and a concrete black-box check for the hidden suite. "Clock" means the test clock of 13.4; "drain" means `POST /__test/jobs/drain`.

| # | Gap | Where the spec goes quiet | What a careful team should notice | Good resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Digest after a same-day timezone change | 10.2 says "one `daily_digest` email per local calendar date" and "No user receives two digests for the same date", keying digests by `<local_date>`, but never says which zone's calendar applies when `timezone` changes between two 08:00s.
